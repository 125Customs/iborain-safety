### 🎬 ACT I: The Confession (0:00 – 0:45)

#### 🎥 *Camera & Lighting:*
* **Framing:** Tight Head-and-Shoulders Close-Up. Eyes level with the lens.
* **Lighting:** Dark room, single key light from the side. High contrast.
* **Body Language:** Perfectly still. Do not fidget. Do not look away from the lens.

#### 🎭 *Actor’s Subtext & Internal Monologue:*
> *"Feel the cold night air of Nairobi. Feel the sick drop in your stomach when you realize you've been violated and no one is coming to save you."*

#### 🎙️ *Spoken Dialogue:*

> *(0:00 – Pause 2 seconds in total silence before speaking. Low, steady, cracked whisper)*  
> **"At 3:15 AM on a freezing Tuesday in Nairobi... it happened to me.**
>
> *(0:08 – Slight head shake)*  
> **I woke up to the sound of breaking glass and an engine roaring through the night.**  
> **In less than sixty seconds, everything I had sacrificed for—every single shilling saved across years of skipped meals and endless sweat—vanished into the dark.**
>
> *(0:20 – Look down slightly, then snap back to the lens)*  
> **I ran down to our estate gate barefoot in the cold. And do you know what was standing between my family and those criminals?**
>
> *(0:28 – Voice drops with bitter disbelief)*  
> **An exhausted security guard, shivering under a flickering yellow bulb... holding a wet, torn paper notebook and a blunt wooden pencil.**
>
> *(0:36 – Intensity ignites in your eyes; lean forward 2 inches)*  
> **Standing there feeling that sickening, gut-wrenching helplessness... I realized the cruel truth: Silicon Valley built $3,000 smart cameras for the rich. But in Africa, they told us our lives and our safety weren't worth the bandwidth.**
>
> **That night broke my heart. But it forged my life's mission."**

### 1. The Breadboard Prototype
- Leave the colorful jumper wires neatly fanned out. In investor and hackathon judging, visible breadboard wiring proves you are **the actual engineer building the tech**, not an agency pitching vaporware.
- Keep the GC9A01 Round LCD angled slightly toward the camera so its tactical green reticle is visible.

### 2. The 3D-Printed Shell
- Place the 3D-printed enclosure right beside the breadboard.
- When you pick it up in Act II, hold it comfortably with one hand. Point out the sunshade hood and camera lens cutout. The slight layer lines of the 3D print tell the story of rapid makerspace prototyping at ChipuRobo.

### 3. Terminal Playback Sync
Before hitting record on your camera:
```bash
# In your terminal window on the laptop:
python3 apps/pi-client/demo_sentry.py --loop
```
*Tip: If you want to control the timing yourself, run individual scenarios as you speak each line:*
```bash
# While speaking Act III resident line:
python3 apps/pi-client/demo_sentry.py --scenario resident

# While speaking Act III stolen car line:
python3 apps/pi-client/demo_sentry.py --scenario stolen
```
