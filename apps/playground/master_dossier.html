1. Executive Summary & The Problem We Solve

In Sub-Saharan Africa, over 70% of violent and property crime is transit-borne (getaway cars with cloned plates, unplated Boda Bodas). Flock Safety costs $5,000/year (pricing out 99% of Africa), while dumb CCTV only records passive video without real-time deterrence. Iborain Safety deploys a $49/mo infrastructure-free public safety grid powered by Google Gemini (Gemini 3.7 Flash & Live API) and locally fabricated edge sentries to eliminate crime in real time.

3. The 3 Pillars of Iborain Safety
Pillar 1 — African Transit Fingerprinting™: Reads muddy plates, identifies vehicle customizations, and profiles Boda Bodas (models, helmet compliance, rider jackets, and cargo such as 13kg gas cylinders).
Pillar 2 — Regional Threat Mesh & Instant WhatsApp Alert: Synchronizes hotlists across a 10km grid with sub-600ms latency, delivering instant photo proof and threat metadata to security patrols.
Pillar 3 — FreeForm™ Natural Language Crime Search: Detectives query multi-day forensic history using plain English or Swahili (e.g. "Show unplated motorbikes carrying gas cylinders between 1 AM and 4 AM").
4. Job Creation & The ChipuRobo Micro-Franchise Flywheel

In direct partnership with ChipuRobo (empowering 4M youth by 2030 through hands-on robotics), Iborain trains young technicians from Kenya's informal sector (Jua Kali) to assemble, mount, and service sentry nodes. Installers earn KES 30,400 to KES 58,000/mo ($233 - $450/mo) managing 10 local sentry nodes—creating high-value, sustainable middle-class careers.
